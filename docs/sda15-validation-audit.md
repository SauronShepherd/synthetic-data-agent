## Bidirectional review result

The article-to-implementation pass found the semantic contract, denial paths, local tests, bundle wiring, and a live Databricks smoke run. The implementation-to-article pass found the allowlist, benchmark IDs, stale/evidence checks, raw-value boundaries, observable denial, and the lack of a live Genie integration represented in the correct sections.

The audit deliberately treats *governed Genie contract* and *Databricks Genie product integration* as different capabilities. Conflating them would make the article sound more impressive and the system less trustworthy.
