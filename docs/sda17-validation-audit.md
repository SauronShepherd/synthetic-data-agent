## Bidirectional review result

The article-to-implementation pass found the read-only snapshot, semantic contract, action allowlist, approval gates, smoke entry point, local tests, and Databricks action receipt. The implementation-to-article pass found those capabilities described without upgrading authorization into execution or future product surfaces into present ones.

The important boundary is deliberate: a control center can coordinate three kinds of evidence without becoming one giant component that monitors, interprets, and mutates everything it can see.
