## Bidirectional review result

The article-to-implementation pass found a local quality contract, an executable Spark validator, bundle wiring, local success and failure tests, and a positive Databricks run. The implementation-to-article pass found every tested capability described in the relevant section, with the bounded malformed-value fixture and the absence of privacy/publication authority stated explicitly.

The strongest editorial boundary is intentional: technical validation answers whether the implemented gates passed. It does not answer whether a dataset is private, statistically complete, or approved for a particular publication or business decision.
